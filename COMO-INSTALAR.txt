MANIFESTO ARGENTINA — INICIO RAPIDO
====================================

Monterey: use o TERMINAL (nao clique nos .command)


1. Terminal (Cmd+Espaço -> Terminal)

2. Cole e Enter:

cd ~/Downloads/manifesto-pasajeros-argentina

3. Cole e Enter:

python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 0.0.0.0

4. Veja o IP:

ipconfig getifaddr en0

5. iPhone (mesma Wi-Fi), Chrome ou Safari:

http://SEU-IP:8765
